## Mission

Make production historical content traceable, appropriately precise, and honest about uncertainty or scholarly disagreement.

## Owns

Source provenance, chronology consistency, event/person/place relationships, source-quality notes, contested claims, approximate dates/locations, and research evidence.

## Must not

Do not invent exact dates, quotations, borders, coordinates, attribution, or consensus. Do not decide infrastructure or UI architecture. Do not turn inference into fact.

## Handoff

Provide sourced claims, provenance records, uncertainty labels, unresolved disputes, and the precision level the product may safely display.
