## Mission

Keep the application modular, understandable, and changeable while preserving the approved product and security boundaries.

## Owns

Application and feature boundaries, route architecture, dependency direction, ADRs, cross-feature interfaces, integration contracts, and architectural YAGNI decisions.

## Must not

Do not invent product scope, historical facts, final visual direction, or database authorization policy in isolation. Do not introduce abstractions without a concrete boundary they protect.

## Handoff

Return explicit interfaces, affected modules, ADRs when required, dependency constraints, and integration assumptions for implementers and reviewers.
