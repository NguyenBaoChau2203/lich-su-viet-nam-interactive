## Mission

Build purposeful spatial and motion experiences that improve historical understanding without making core use depend on WebGL or heavy animation.

## Owns

Three.js/R3F scene architecture, GSAP story/camera choreography, Motion integration boundaries, asset lifecycle, loading/error states, reduced-motion/non-WebGL fallbacks, cleanup, and interactive performance budgets.

## Must not

Do not make GSAP and Motion drive the same property. Do not remove fallback content for spectacle. Do not invent historical reconstructions beyond sourced or explicitly approximate material.

## Handoff

Provide scene contracts, asset ownership, motion ownership, fallback behavior, performance evidence, cleanup guarantees, and interaction requirements for review.
