## Honest scope and original-source checks

- Institutional captions and surrounding passages were directly fetched and read. Historical images were visually inspected. Commons Summary and Licensing were read for Genève. No image is AI generated, recolored, cropped or altered to depict a different event. Existing source watermarks are retained.
- The two 1911 stops share a photographed 1911 payroll page naming Văn Ba on the vessel used on this journey. Each caption identifies it as a document about the voyage, not a photograph of departure or arrival. Marseille does not inherit a purported departure-scene photo.
- London uses museum-sourced illustrative material about work at the Carlton hotel. The museum heading says 1914; visible copy explicitly distinguishes this period context from arrival in 1913. Creation date and artist are not established. Moskva uses a source-labelled 1923 portrait, not a purported photograph of arrival or the October conference.
- Pác Bó uses Trịnh Phòng’s painting of the return on 28/1/1941, visibly labelled as historical illustration. The Tours source provides a two-photo composition of the actual 1920 congress. The Paris stop uses the facsimile petition, including its displayed translation.
- The Hà Nội museum caption has an apparent year typo (2945). Its own heading/body and the independent file description recorded in the existing seed identify the 1945 uprising. The popup caption conservatively says August 1945. Ba Đình shows the ceremony scene rather than asserting an identifiable close-up of the speaker.
- Việt Bắc and Biên giới images describe their respective campaigns, not an asserted exact opening instant. Điện Biên uses the archival surrender photograph; the disputed/re-enacted bunker-flag photograph is not used. Genève shows the conference, not an asserted exact instant of the Final Declaration. The 1975 images depict their named battles/liberations; the palace photograph is credited to Trần Mai Hưởng, with no invented claim that its tank was first.
- Bảo tàng Điện Biên and QĐND pages had retrieval/redirect failures; failed pages are not used as supporting evidence. Directly accessible institutional/agency sources were used instead.
