## C1 — P2: A restored close camera is not applied to a newly attached area layer

Locations: `lib/explorer/cinematic/scene.ts:492–509`; `lib/explorer/cinematic/province-areas.ts:27,32–46,323–328`.

`setProvinceAreas()` builds the layer asynchronously, then assigns it and adds its group. It does not initialize that layer with the already-current pose/near plane. The layer starts with `cameraRadius = Infinity` and `effectiveLift = 0.006`, so its selected cap can mount at radius 1.0064 even though an initial direct URL already placed the camera closer than that. Clearance updates made before the layer existed were lost; the next camera interaction may change the result, but a saved view must be safe before that interaction.

Actual direct-entry reproductions at 1280×720, with reduced motion, after `data-province-areas=ready` and no user camera interaction:

| URL view | Mounted cap radius | Camera radius at this top-down view | Result |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| `ban-do=21.028,105.854,11` | 1.0064 | ~1.0058476425 | Camera is inside the selected Hà Nội cap. |
| `ban-do=21.028,105.854,18` | 1.0064 | ~1.0000456847 | Camera is inside the cap; even the constant 1.0004 base would be above the camera if lift were zero. |

The complete test URLs also include `noi-dung=dia-phuong&dia-phuong=ha-noi`. The browser preserves the accepted zoom in `scene.getView()`. Camera radii above are derived from the existing top-down camera equations, FOV and actual stage height; actual cap vertex min/max radii corroborate the 1.0064 top surface. This is a direct-entry failure, not a test that bypasses URL restoration by inventing a different camera API.

Evidence: `.task-reports/f1-f5-rereview-20261029/restored-view11-probe.json`, `restored-view-probe.json`, `restored-zoom11.png`, `restored-zoom18.png` and their diagnostic scripts.

Required correction:

1. Initialize a newly built layer from the latest existing camera pose/near plane before mounting/selecting/drawing it. Retain epoch/disposal checks and handle camera changes during asynchronous construction.
2. Define a safe base/lift/depth policy for every accepted restored view. Merely clamping lift cannot protect a constant base already above the camera. Keep cap, wall bottoms/tops, outline, pick surface and reported audit values coherent.
3. Preserve URL/camera/mode/selected code/cards. Do not fix the test by silently discarding the saved zoom or adding a second camera owner. If a geometry-only precision fallback is necessary, qualify it explicitly and restore physical lift when the view allows.
4. Add initial-load and delayed-load regressions at zoom 11 and legacy 18 on all four desktops, both motion settings, before any resize/wheel/zoom interaction; retain F1–F5 and X behavior.
