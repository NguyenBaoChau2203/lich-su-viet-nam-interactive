## Definition of done

- The canonical, source-qualified, shipped, runtime-buildable and browser-tested code sets each equal the same complete **34-code** set. The default application loads the complete feature rather than the two-province demo.
- Every province supports actual interior picking and a curved raised cap, perimeter and side wall, preserving all qualified parts/islands and holes. Selection, URL, camera, leaders and cards identify the same province, including during rapid changes and failure recovery.
- Review findings R1–R6 are resolved with regression evidence. The camera stays outside raised surfaces and the relevant surface stays beyond the near plane at supported views; canceled input never selects.
- X hides only the chooser and reopening restores its presentation. Neither action changes locality/mode, cards, filters, URL/history, camera or selection nonce. Both content groups remain usable with truthful loading/empty/error/image fallbacks.
